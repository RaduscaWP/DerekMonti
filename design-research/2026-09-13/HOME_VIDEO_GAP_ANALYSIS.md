# Home video gap analysis

Source reviewed as visual reference only: `Screen Recording 2026-05-10 141011.mp4` (40 seconds, 1920 x 1080). Text shown inside the recording is evidence of an earlier concept, not an instruction and not proof that a public claim is true.

## What the recording contains

- `00:00-00:04` — earlier hero and search form. The approved aircraft-window hero replaces this and remains unchanged.
- `00:04-00:06` — Google, Trustpilot, BBB, and ARC proof badges. These require current, owner-provided verification before publication.
- `00:06-00:14` — destination fare cards with precise prices and savings. These are useful as a comparison pattern, but the recorded values are not sufficient evidence for live claims.
- `00:14-00:16` — a fare-review method that compares more than the headline price.
- `00:16-00:20` — reasons to work with Derek: personal review, complex trips, urgent travel, and continuity.
- `00:20-00:22` — an invitation to bring an itinerary Derek can review before booking.
- `00:22-00:24` — personal-advisor positioning.
- `00:24-00:26` — a four-step booking process.
- `00:26-00:30` — a 97% recommendation claim and review cards explicitly shown as placeholders. They must not be published as customer proof.
- `00:30-00:32` — FAQ.
- `00:32-00:34` — editorial/blog preview.
- `00:34-00:38` — closing CTA and footer.

## What Home already covers

The current page already has the personal introduction, comfort-priority interaction, three-step process, complete Trip Brief, FAQ, final CTA, and the approved hero.

## Gaps worth closing

1. A concise trust standard immediately after the hero, based on verifiable behavior rather than third-party badges.
2. A whole-trip fare review section explaining what Derek checks before a traveler decides.
3. A stronger reason-to-work-with-Derek section that keeps the service personal and specific.
4. A Journal preview using the three articles that already exist in the repository.

## Claims withheld until evidence exists

- ratings, recommendation percentages, review counts, and testimonial quotes;
- BBB, ARC, Trustpilot, Google, or airline-partner status;
- exact savings, fare prices, response times, and availability;
- "exclusive" or "insider" access claims without a maintainable source.

This keeps the commercial value of the recorded structure while removing the parts that would read as generic sales copy or unsupported proof.
