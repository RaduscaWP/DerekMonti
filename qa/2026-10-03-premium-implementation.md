# Implementation record — 3 October 2026

## Visual thesis

An aviation website with Derek visibly at its center: the genuine portrait opens the story on navy, oversized Syne typography gives it a distinct rhythm, and white, navy and burgundy alternate around real trip decisions. The actual portrait remains in its original setting. Destination cards retain their ticket construction and 3D response. The user clarified that the mandatory hero image is the portrait from the concept, replacing the earlier aircraft-window interpretation.

## Content plan

Seven chapters: the retained hero and route entry; a traveler-priority review; route cards and service access; Derek and the review process; the real request form; practical questions; the journal. Navigation and footer share one visual system on every route. No fictional testimonials, biography, availability or savings claims.

## Motion plan

1. A staggered opening and restrained section reveals, without scroll interception or pinned content.
2. Priority imagery crossfades and its explanation follows an explicit choice. Only Apply changes the real brief.
3. Pointer-driven 3D ticket cards, service flips, image parallax and link feedback. Native scrolling and a visible motion control remain available; reduced motion keeps every control and piece of content usable.
