# Questionnaire for Derek — facts the website needs (October 2026)

Notă pentru Radu: trimite-i lui Derek lista de mai jos. Fiecare răspuns deblochează un element precis de pe site (în paranteză). Până primim răspunsurile, site-ul folosește cifrele din brief (15–60%, 20+ clienți, 24/7) marcate ca exemple, fără recenzii inventate.

Please answer what you can; short answers are fine. Each item says what it unlocks on the site.

## 1. Real bookings for the route cards (unlocks: savings cards, hero numbers, FAQ examples)
For 3–8 recent bookings, give: route (e.g. JFK–LHR), cabin (business/first), airline(s), the airline's published fare at the time, the fare the client paid, and the month. Anonymous, no client names.

## 2. Reviews (unlocks: testimonials section, /reviews page)
- Links to any public review profiles (Trustpilot, Google, Facebook) in your name or the agency's.
- 3–5 clients who agreed to be quoted: first name + initial, city, route, one or two sentences, and the channel where they wrote it (WhatsApp, email, Google).

## 3. Your background (unlocks: About page bio, counters, timeline)
- Year you started in aviation / airfares, and the year you took your first private client.
- Approximate number of clients you serve today, and how many are repeat clients.
- Where you are based (Miami confirmed?) and the languages you speak with clients.
- One or two sentences on how you get access to consolidator fares (what can be said publicly).
- A second photo: you at work, at an airport, or with a boarding pass (landscape, high resolution).

## 4. Airlines (unlocks: "Airlines I book" strip, airline pages)
Which airlines you ticket most often, and whether any formal partnership exists that may be named.

## 5. Response time and hours (unlocks: hero promise, form success message, 24/7 claim)
- Realistic reply time for a quote (e.g. "within 2 hours, 8am–10pm EST", "same day").
- Hours you actually answer WhatsApp. Is "24/7" accurate, or "24/7 for travelling clients"?

## 6. Travel Business Class affiliation (unlocks: trust bar, footer line, schema)
- Exact wording you want on the site (current draft: "Tickets are issued through Travel Business Class, an ARC-accredited agency").
- ARC number, BBB profile and Trustpilot profile of the agency, and confirmation that we may show its ratings next to your name.
- Should the contact email stay Derek@travelbusinessclass.com, or do you want derek@flywithderek.com?

## 7. Pricing model (unlocks: "How pricing works" FAQ)
Is the quote free? Do you charge a fee, a percentage of savings, or earn a commission from the fare? One sentence we can publish.

## 8. Local presence (unlocks: Google Business Profile, Miami page)
Do you meet clients in person or serve a defined area (Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach)? Do you have a business address we can use for Google Business Profile (it can stay hidden)?

## 9. Social profiles (unlocks: schema sameAs, footer links)
LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook or other profiles to link from the site.
